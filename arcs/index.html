Arcs

Five selections. One project. From Building 5 to the Neo Egoist League.

  1. Selection 01

    First Selection

    Building 5 · Teams V–Z

    Three hundred strikers, five buildings, five teams of eleven. Survive a round-robin or go home ordinary.

  2. Selection 02

    Second Selection

    3v3 · 4v4 · 5v5

    Teams dissolve. Pick a rival, steal their weapons, and climb a tower of rooms where losing means restarting from zero.

  3. Selection 03

    Third Selection

    Team Red / Team White · Tryouts

    The remaining egoists are mixed into high-level 5v5s, then tried out for the Eleven that will face Japan.

  4. Selection 04

    U-20 Match

    Blue Lock Eleven vs Japan U-20

    Ego wagers the entire project on one match. Sae Itoshi comes home. Japan watches.

  5. Selection 05

    Neo Egoist League

    U-20 club league · Europe

    Blue Lock’s survivors are drafted into five European clubs and ranked by what the world will pay for them.
