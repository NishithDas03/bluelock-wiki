About Blue Lock

Story Overview

Blue Lock is a Japanese manga series written by Muneyuki Kaneshiro and illustrated by Yusuke Nomura. The story follows Yoichi Isagi, a high school soccer player who joins the Blue Lock project, a controversial training program designed to create Japan's greatest striker.

The Blue Lock Project

After Japan's disappointing performance in the World Cup, the Japan Football Association decides to implement an unprecedented training program. 300 high school strikers are gathered in a prison-like facility where they must compete against each other in various challenges and matches.
